Installation on macOS

Unzip the download in Finder and choose the format you want to install.

Standalone

Drag NAMplified.app into Applications and open it from there.

On first launch, allow microphone access so NAMplified can receive your guitar signal. In Audio / MIDI Settings, select your interface, guitar input channel, and output. With an interface or headphones connected, uncheck Mute audio input.

If macOS blocks the first launch and you trust the download source:

  1. Dismiss the warning.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Under Security, click Open Anyway and confirm.

If the button is missing, try opening the app again and return to Privacy & Security. Apple Support

VST3 and Audio Unit

Quit your DAW. In Finder, press ⌘ ⇧ G to open the destination folder, then copy the plug-in into it.

  • VST3 · Copy NAMplified.vst3 to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3.
  • AU · Copy NAMplified.component to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components.

Reopen your DAW and look for NAMplified. If it does not appear, rescan plug-ins.
